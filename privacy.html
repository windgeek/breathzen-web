Privacy Policy for BreathZen
Effective August 10, 2026

Account

Practice

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data through BreathZen. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or third-party account system.

Data stored on your device

Your breathing patterns, settings, purchase-entitlement cache, and practice history are stored locally on your device. BreathZen does not upload them to a developer-operated server.

Deleting the app removes its local app data. Apple Health entries, if any, are managed separately in the Health app.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional. If you grant permission, BreathZen writes completed practices to Apple Health as mindful sessions. BreathZen does not read Health data, and the developer does not receive Health data.

Purchases

Purchases and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process information under Apple's Privacy Policy. BreathZen receives only the transaction information required to determine whether the non-consumable feature is unlocked.

Opening purchase features, buying, or restoring may contact Apple's App Store services. Everyday breathing practice does not require a network connection.

Audio and microphone

Breathing guidance is generated on your device from audio material bundled with the app. BreathZen does not request microphone permission, record your voice, or listen to your breathing.

System services and children

BreathZen may use standard Apple system services such as StoreKit, Apple Health, audio playback, haptics, and local storage. Their operation is governed by your device settings and Apple's terms.

BreathZen is not directed at children for the purpose of collecting personal information, and the developer knowingly collects no personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text and effective date will be published on this page. Material changes will reflect changes in the app's actual behavior.

开发者收集什么

不收集任何个人数据。开发者不会通过 BreathZen 收集、传输、出售、出租或共享个人数据。App 不包含广告 SDK、分析统计 SDK、跨 App 追踪或第三方账号系统。

存储在设备上的数据

呼吸法、设置、购买权益缓存和练习记录存储在你的设备本地，不会上传到开发者运营的服务器。

删除 App 会移除它的本地数据。如有 Apple 健康记录，请在“健康”App 中单独管理。

Apple 健康

Apple 健康完全可选。经你授权后，BreathZen 会将完成的练习作为正念时间写入 Apple 健康。BreathZen 不读取健康数据，开发者也不会收到健康数据。

购买

购买和恢复购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。Apple 可能依据其隐私政策处理相关信息。BreathZen 只获得判断非消耗型功能是否已解锁所必需的交易信息。

打开购买功能、购买或恢复时可能连接 Apple 的 App Store 服务；日常呼吸练习不需要网络连接。

声音与麦克风

呼吸引导由 App 内置的声音素材在设备本地生成。BreathZen 不会请求麦克风权限，不录制你的声音，也不会监听你的呼吸。

系统服务与儿童隐私

BreathZen 会使用 StoreKit、Apple 健康、音频播放、触感和本地存储等 Apple 标准系统服务，其运行受你的设备设置和 Apple 条款约束。

BreathZen 不以收集个人信息为目的面向儿童，开发者也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如本政策发生变化，更新后的内容与生效日期会发布在本页；任何实质变化都会与 App 的实际行为保持一致。
