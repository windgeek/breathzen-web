# Multilingual support and privacy QA — October 5, 2026

## Coverage

Both pages cover en, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ja, ko, de, fr, es and pt-BR.
The source and generated HTML contain matching locale sets and complete content.
Both existing public paths are retained. CSS/JS URLs carry a deterministic
content hash to prevent clients using the previous two-language script.

## Language review

The nine versions were compared against the English source for meaning,
terminology, commerce, safety and privacy. The review specifically preserved:

- Nonverbal, on-device breathing audio; no microphone access or recording.
- Free editing and previews; one non-consumable purchase for unlimited saving;
  no subscription and no promised cloud sync.
- Device-local legacy unlock, with no promise of transfer to another device.
- Optional, write-only Health integration, unavailable on Mac; no developer
  receipt of Health data.
- Offline daily practice, with Apple connectivity for purchase and restoration.
- No developer collection, transmission, sale, rental or sharing through the
  app; no advertising/analytics SDK, cross-app tracking or third-party account.
- Separate Health record management; Mac uninstall may leave local app data.
- Stop if dizzy, strained, short of breath or unwell; no diagnosis/treatment.
- Both existing support emails and original policy effective date.

Restore instructions were aligned with the current app's Settings footer and
purchase screen. Restore labels match Localizable.xcstrings. Locale-specific
half-second punctuation and Traditional Chinese wording were reviewed.
The October 5 note describes translations/platform clarification, not a new
collection practice or an app behavior change.

## Browser checks

Actual browser checks covered support and privacy in all nine locales at
1280px desktop and 375px mobile widths. Both pages were also loaded directly
at 320px, checking the active locale, page title, expected section count and
horizontal overflow. A German privacy heading overflow discovered at 320px
was fixed with a short localized page heading; the complete policy title is
retained in the page content and document title. All nine privacy headings
were rechecked at 320px, and all eighteen desktop pages were rechecked.

Language switches and support/privacy round trips were exercised. The site
retains readable static articles and anchor navigation without JavaScript;
this fallback was checked in source rather than with JavaScript disabled in
the browser. No app binary or App Store metadata was changed in this task.
